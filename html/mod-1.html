RECORD TABLE

3D Model

Photoshop (Concept Art) | Blender (Model/Lighting/Render/Compositing) | InDesign (Font/Typography for Poster Version)

3D model for Teatro de Yale's production of 21 Chump Street. Poster for the performance.

After creating the poster, I reused the lamp to make a comforting space with a record player and tea to test out volumetric lighting. Later turned into a lo-fi fan-edit with BTS' Jhope's album "Hope World" as the featured vinyl record!
